**Research Data, Workbooks, and Outputs**

This repository contains selected workbooks, datasets, analysis files, experimental outputs, and supporting materials associated with my research on trust assessment for AI-generated outputs.

The materials in this repository support work presented in, or developed as part of, the following research papers:

**Publications and Research Papers**
**Operationalization of Trust in AI Outputs: A Multi-Signal, Task-Aware Trust Scoring Framework**

Published in **IEEE IntelliSys, September 2026.**

This research explores the operationalization of trust in AI-generated outputs through a multi-signal, task-aware framework. The work investigates how different trust-related signals can be combined and interpreted to produce more meaningful assessments of AI outputs across different tasks and contexts.

Selected workbooks, data, calculations, experiments, and resulting outputs related to this research are included in this repository.

**When Is An Output-Level Trust Score Meaningful?**

**_Upcoming submission to Elsevier._**

This research extends the investigation into AI output-level trust assessment by examining the conditions under which a single trust score is meaningful, interpretable, and useful. It considers the limitations of aggregating multiple trust signals and explores how task context, signal relationships, and uncertainty affect the interpretation of an output-level trust score.

Supporting experimental data, workbooks, analyses, and outputs associated with this research may also be found in this repository.

Repository Contents

Depending on the research experiment or analysis, the repository may contain:

Experimental datasets
Analysis workbooks and spreadsheets

Trust-signal measurements

Experimental results

Supporting research outputs and validation materials
